Biography

Merily Leotoots is a violinist whose work is defined by precision, structural focus, and a direct interpretive voice. Her playing favours clarity of line, disciplined ensemble awareness, and a balanced intensity that avoids excess.

She studied at the Tallinn Music High School and the Estonian Academy of Music and Theatre with Professor Mari Tampere-Bezrodny and Sigrid Kuulmann, later refining her technique in London, Vienna, and through private studies with Pierre Amoyal. Further development came through masterclasses with leading European violinists and participation in specialised programmes, including the Sarasate Academy.

Her core field is chamber music. She performs with Duo EnPassant, a long-standing violin–cello partnership; with the piano trio Trio Callistó; and in the piano quartet Quattuor Artium, with whom she appeared at the Wye Valley Chamber Music Festival, giving the world premiere of Artur Lemba’s Piano Quartet. Her approach has been shaped by mentors from both classical and historically-informed traditions.

Her work extends across Estonia and Europe, including engagements in France, Austria, Germany, the United Kingdom, Greece, Spain, and Ireland. Alongside performance, she develops projects that link narrative and music, drawing on historical, literary, and contemporary material to build programmes with clear conceptual framing.
